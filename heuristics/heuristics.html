    Usability heuristics

    Heuristics are broad rules of thumb for interface design. Tagging feedback with one explains why something is a problem, not just what to change, and makes patterns visible on the board.

    Foxio uses Jakob Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics, which are widely used in design reviews, plus Accessibility based on WCAG. Each section below explains the principle, what violations typically look like, and how to fix them. Where the automated audit checks a heuristic, the checks are listed.

    Severity and priority

    The audit rates each finding by severity, which becomes the card’s priority on the board. Use the same scale when you set the priority of your own comments.

    PriorityAudit severityMeaning
    CriticalBlockerStops people completing a key task, or excludes some users entirely. Fix before release.
    HighMajorCauses significant difficulty, errors or frustration. Fix soon.
    MediumMinorSlows people down or annoys them, but they get there. Schedule it.
    LowCosmeticPolish. Fix when there is time.

    Based on Nielsen’s severity ratings, which weigh how often a problem occurs, how much it hurts when it does, and whether users can work around it.
